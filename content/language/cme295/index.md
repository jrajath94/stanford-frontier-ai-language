The applied companion to CS336. Nine lectures that take you from the transformer to agentic LLMs, taught by the Amidi brothers with a practitioner's eye.

CME295 overlaps heavily with CS336, so these lessons are bridges: they link to CS336 for the deep mechanics and teach only what is new here, the Amidi framing, practical insights, and exam-style material.

> [!CAVEAT] Primary source is Autumn 2025 (complete, 9 lectures). Autumn 2026 is running now with a restructured syllabus (RL with LLMs, LLM systems, diffusion LLMs as separate lectures); only its Lecture 1 is published. Differences are noted where relevant.

## Lessons

1. [Transformers](l01-transformers.html) — tokenization to the full architecture, end to end
2. [Transformer-based models and tricks](l02-models-tricks.html) — MHA/MQA/GQA, RoPE, BERT family
3. [Large language models](l03-llms.html) — MoE, sampling, in-context learning, chain-of-thought
4. [LLM training](l04-training.html) — pretraining, SFT, LoRA, quantization
5. [LLM tuning](l05-tuning.html) — RLHF, reward modeling, PPO, DPO
6. [LLM reasoning](l06-reasoning.html) — reasoning models, RL training, GRPO
7. [Agentic LLMs](l07-agents.html) — RAG, function calling, ReAct
8. [LLM evaluation](l08-evaluation.html) — LLM-as-a-judge, biases, benchmarks
9. [Recap and current trends](l09-trends.html) — the arc, where the field heads
