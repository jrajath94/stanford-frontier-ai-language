CME295: Transformers and Large Language Models

Instructor: Afshine Amidi, Shervine Amidi

The applied companion to CS336. Nine lectures that take you from the transformer to agentic LLMs, taught by the Amidi brothers with a practitioner’s eye.

CME295 overlaps heavily with CS336, so these lessons are bridges: they link to CS336 for the deep mechanics and teach only what is new here, the Amidi framing, practical insights, and exam-style material.

Caveat

Primary source is Autumn 2025 (complete, 9 lectures). Autumn 2026 is running now with a restructured syllabus (RL with LLMs, LLM systems, diffusion LLMs as separate lectures); only its Lecture 1 is published. Differences are noted where relevant.

Lessons

  1. Transformers — tokenization to the full architecture, end to end
  2. Transformer-based models and tricks — MHA/MQA/GQA, RoPE, BERT family
  3. Large language models — MoE, sampling, in-context learning, chain-of-thought
  4. LLM training — pretraining, SFT, LoRA, quantization
  5. LLM tuning — RLHF, reward modeling, PPO, DPO
  6. LLM reasoning — reasoning models, RL training, GRPO
  7. Agentic LLMs — RAG, function calling, ReAct
  8. LLM evaluation — LLM-as-a-judge, biases, benchmarks
  9. Recap and current trends — the arc, where the field heads
